1. Price ≤ Bollinger Band Lower (20, 2) — stock is oversold
2. RSI(14) < 35 — momentum confirms oversold
3. CCI(20) < -100 — extreme negative deviation
4. GARCH volatility forecast is NOT rising — volatility not accelerating (safe to buy)

The dual-exit strategy's drawdown is **one-third** of the alternatives. This is critical for:
- **Psychological sustainability** — easier to hold during drawdowns
- **Capital preservation** — less capital to recover from losses
- **Compounding protection** — drawdowns compound non-linearly (50% loss requires 100% gain to recover)

### 9.2 Key Risk Factors

1. **2008-style crash:** The 20-year price range (23-318) shows LGEN has survived major crashes. However, extreme volatility spikes could overwhelm the GARCH filter. Mitigation: the ATR trail provides a hard stop.

2. **Extended bear market:** If LGEN enters a secular downtrend (like 2007-2011), BB mean-reversion entries would repeatedly catch falling knives. Mitigation: the GARCH filter avoids entries when volatility is rising, and the RSI/CCI thresholds ensure deep oversold conditions.

3. **Low signal environment:** In strong uptrends, BB Lower signals may never fire. The 2016 data shows the strategy had no signals in that year. Mitigation: this is not a risk per se — the strategy only acts when opportunities exist.

## 10. Implementation Guidance

### 10.1 Entry Checklist (execute on trading day)

```
✓ Close ≤ Bollinger Band Lower (20, 2)
✓ RSI(14) < 35
✓ CCI(20) < -100
✓ ta_garch_vol ≤ yesterday's ta_garch_vol  (volatility not rising)
```

**If all 4 conditions are true:** Enter long position with 100% of available cash.

### 10.2 Exit Rules (monitor daily)

```
WHEN ANY of the following triggers:
  1. Close ≥ Bollinger Band Middle (20) → EXIT (take profit)
  OR
  2. Close < (5-day High − 3 × ATR(14)) → EXIT (stop loss / trail)
```

### 10.3 Position Sizing

- **Size:** 100% of available cash per trade (as backtested)
- **Commission:** Budget 0.1% per entry + 0.1% per exit
- **Maximum concurrent positions:** 1 (LGEN.L only)
- **Minimum cash buffer:** 2% of portfolio for slippage

### 10.4 Monitoring & Review

- **Review entry signals:** Weekly (check if conditions are being met)
- **Review exit triggers:** Daily (ensure trailing stop is functioning)
- **Quarterly review:** Re-evaluate GARCH filter effectiveness
- **Annual review:** Verify that BB/RSI/CCI parameters remain optimal
