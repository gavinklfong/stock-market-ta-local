## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. bb_breakout — BB Breakout ⭐ BEST OVERALL

**Logic:** Enters when price breaks above the upper Bollinger Band, confirming the breakout with rising volatility and positive momentum. Exits when price falls back below the middle band or MACD turns bearish.

**Entry conditions:**
- Close > BB Upper (price breaks above 2σ band)
- ATR(14) rising (volatility expanding)
- HV(20) rising (high volume relative to recent average)
- ROC(20) > 0 (20-day momentum positive)

**Exit conditions:**
- Close < BB Middle (breakout failed, price reverts to middle band)
- MACD line < MACD signal (momentum turning bearish)

### 2. bb_meanrev_atr — BB MeanRev (ATR Trail)

**Logic:** Buys when price dips to the lower Bollinger Band with oversold RSI, uses ATR trailing stop to lock in gains.

**Entry conditions:**
- Close ≤ BB Lower
- RSI(14) < 35

**Exit conditions:**
- Close ≥ BB Middle (minimum exit)
- Close < 5-day high - 4×ATR(14) (ATR trailing stop)

**Results:** +8.27% return, 0.61 Sharpe, 10.22% max DD, 5 trades, 80% win rate

**Analysis:** Captured 5 dips during the uptrend but each was a small bounce (avg win +3.56%). The 4th trade was a loser (-5.85%) when the pullback deepened. The ATR trail didn't significantly improve exits — the BB Middle exit triggered first in most cases.

### 3. dual_exit_meanrev — Dual-Exit MeanRev

**Logic:** Same as bb_meanrev_atr but with an additional CCI filter (< -100) for entry and a tighter 3-day rolling ATR trail.

**Entry conditions:**

**Exit conditions:**

**Results:** +8.27% return, 0.61 Sharpe, identical to bb_meanrev_atr

**Analysis:** The CCI filter and tighter ATR trail produced identical results — the CCI was redundant (BB Lower + RSI < 35 was already a strong oversold signal), and the 3-day vs 5-day rolling window had no practical difference in this uptrend.

### 4. bb_meanrev — BB Mean Reversion

### 5. vwap — VWAP Trend

**Logic:** Stays in positions as long as price stays above the rolling VWAP, with RSI > 50 confirmation.

**Exit conditions:**

However, the 4 big wins (including +12.80% and +9.81%) carried the overall return. The 18.11% max drawdown is concerning — nearly double the B&H drawdown in this period.

## Phase 4: Custom Strategies

Created 4 custom breakout strategies in `app/backtest/custom/sbry_breakout.py` designed to improve on bb_breakout by adding volume confirmation, ATR trailing exits, and multi-signal scoring.

### sbry_volume_breakout — Volume-Confirmed BB Breakout

**Logic:** Same entry as bb_breakout but adds:
- CMF > 0 (Chaikin Money Flow positive — real buying pressure)
- Volume > 20-day average (volume surge on breakout)

**Exit conditions:** Same as bb_breakout (Close < BB Middle OR MACD bearish cross)

**Results:** +17.34% return, 1.44 Sharpe, 6.88% max DD, 3 trades, 100% win rate

**Analysis:** The volume filter reduced trade frequency from 7 to 3 but maintained a 100% win rate. All 3 breakouts that passed the volume filter were genuine buying signals. However, the filter was too strict — it missed 4 valid breakouts that bb_breakout captured (including the +16.26% winner). Sharpe of 1.44 is solid but the lower total return means less capital capture.

### sbry_atr_trail_breakout — ATR Trailing Exit Breakout

**Logic:** Same entry as bb_breakout, but replaces the simple BB Middle exit with a 10-day high - 3×ATR trailing stop.

**Entry conditions:** Same as bb_breakout

**Exit conditions:**
- Close < BB Middle (early exit)
- Close < 10-day high - 3×ATR(14) (ATR trailing stop)
- MACD bearish cross

**Results:** +27.36% return, 1.77 Sharpe — **identical to bb_breakout**

**Analysis:** The ATR trail produced identical results because the BB Middle exit and MACD exit triggered first in all 7 trades. The 10-day high - 3×ATR trail never hit before the existing exit conditions. This suggests the existing exits were already effective at the right time, and a wider trailing stop would be needed to test the ATR approach in a longer uptrend.

### sbry_double_breakout — Double-Close Breakout

**Logic:** Requires 2 consecutive closes above BB Upper before entering. This filters out single-day spike breakouts.

**Entry conditions:**
- 2 consecutive days of Close > BB Upper
- ATR rising + HV rising

**Exit conditions:** Same as bb_breakout

**Results:** +3.15% return, 0.36 Sharpe, 10.41% max DD, 4 trades, 33.3% win rate

**Analysis:** Too conservative for this period. Only 4 trades (3 losing) out of 20 months. The double-close requirement filtered out many valid breakouts that were followed by pullbacks. In a strong uptrend, waiting for 2 consecutive closes means entering later and getting less upside.

### sbry_multi_breakout — Multi-Signal Breakout Scoring

**Logic:** Combines trend (30pts), momentum (25pts), volume (25pts), and structure (20pts) into a score. Entry requires score ≥ 70.

**Scoring:**
- Trend: EMA20>EMA50 (10), EMA50>EMA200 (10), SuperTrend+1 (10)
- Momentum: RSI>55 (8), MACD>Signal (8), ROC>0 (5), CCI>0 (4)
- Volume: CMF>0 (10), OBV rising (10), Vol>20d avg (5)
- Structure: BB Upper breakout (20)

**Exit conditions:** Score < 50 OR Close < BB Middle OR MACD bearish cross

**Results:** -5.59% return, -0.31 Sharpe, 13.25% max DD, 16 trades, 43.8% win rate

**Analysis:** The multi-score approach underperformed. The high threshold (≥70) was met too frequently (16 trades vs bb_breakout's 7), indicating the scoring system was too loose when many trend signals were simultaneously true. In a strong uptrend, nearly all trend and momentum conditions are perpetually positive, diluting the scoring system's ability to discriminate. The strategy also had a large loss of -7.80% that dragged down returns.

## Phase 6: Final Recommendation

### Recommended Strategy: **bb_breakout**

**Total Return:** +27.36% | **Sharpe:** 1.77 | **Max DD:** 7.63% | **Trades:** 7

### Strategy Rules

**Entry (all conditions must be true):**
1. Close > Upper Bollinger Band (20-period, 2σ)
2. ATR(14) > ATR(14) shifted 1 day (volatility expanding)
3. HV(20) > HV(20) shifted 1 day (volume expanding)
4. ROC(20) > 0 (positive 20-day momentum)

**Exit (first condition triggers):**
1. Close < Middle Bollinger Band (breakout failed)
2. MACD line < MACD signal line (momentum turned bearish)

### Risk Management

- **Position sizing:** Equal-weight; no leverage
- **Maximum concurrent exposure:** 1 position at a time (SBRY.L single-stock)
- **Stop loss:** Implicit via exit rules — avg loss is only -0.92%, far below any explicit stop would suggest
- **Commission & slippage:** 0.1% each already factored into results
- **Capital allocation:** Recommended ≤10% of portfolio for a single-stock strategy

### When This Strategy Fails

This strategy is **regime-dependent** and will underperform or lose money when:

1. **Sideways/choppy markets:** BB breakouts in range-bound stocks produce whipsaws (buy high, sell low repeatedly). This is why traditional mean-reversion strategies dominate in the 10-year analysis.
2. **Bear markets:** In a sustained downtrend, BB Upper breakouts become "bull traps" — price briefly spikes above the band then reverses hard.
3. **Low-volatility environments:** If ATR is consistently flat and HV never rises, entry conditions will rarely be met, and the strategy will miss rallies entirely.
4. **Short analysis windows:** With fewer than 3–4 months of data, there may be insufficient BB Upper breakouts to generate a meaningful sample.

### When to Use vs. Buy & Hold

| Scenario | bb_breakout | Buy & Hold |
|----------|------------|------------|
| Strong uptrend (current) | ✅ Slightly behind B&H return, but much better risk-adjusted | ✅ Highest absolute return |
| Sideways market | ❌ Loses money on whipsaws | ✅ Neutral |
| Bear market | ✅ Limited exposure (out before deep losses) | ❌ Captures full drawdown |
| High volatility | ✅ Cuts losses on reversals | ❌ Ride full rollercoaster |
| Low volatility | ❌ Too few signals | ✅ Collects dividends |

**Bottom line:** Use bb_breakout when you believe the stock is in a trending regime (confirmed by higher highs and rising moving averages). Switch to B&Hold when the trend is ambiguous and the stock is in a long-term accumulation phase.

### Comparison to 10-Year "Best" Strategy

The 10-year analysis recommended bb_meanrev (65.71% total return). That strategy is **inappropriate** for the current uptrend regime. Using bb_meanrev during this period would yield only +7.13% (bb_meanrev) or +8.27% (bb_meanrev_atr) — significantly underperforming both the B&H benchmark (+28.06%) and bb_breakout (+27.36%).

**The key lesson:** Always match the strategy to the current market regime. No single strategy works across all periods and conditions.
