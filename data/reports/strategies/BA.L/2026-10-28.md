## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. bb_meanrev_upper — BB MeanRev (Upper Exit)
- **Total Return:** 56.89% | **Sharpe:** 0.51 | **Max DD:** 31.93% | **Trades:** 13 | **Win Rate:** 84.6%
- **Logic:** Classic mean-reversion entry — price touches Bollinger Band Lower while both RSI(14) < 30 and Williams %R < -80 confirm deep oversold conditions. Exit when price reaches the Bollinger Band **Upper** (vs. middle band for standard mean-reversion), capturing the full range of the oscillation.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - `Close <= BB Lower`
  - `RSI(14) < 30`
  - `Williams %R < -80`
- **Exit conditions:**
  - `Close >= BB Upper`
- **Why it works for BA.L:** BA.L has wide Bollinger Bands (~12% width), allowing substantial mean-reversion swings from lower to upper band. The defense sector's tendency to overshoot on contract news creates reliable reversion opportunities. The wider exit band (upper vs. middle) captures ~2× more of each oscillation cycle.
- **Caveat:** Only 13 trades in 10 years — the signal is rare and results may not generalize. The 31.93% max drawdown is the highest among top-5 performers.

### 2. bb_breakout — BB Breakout
- **Total Return:** 50.58% | **Sharpe:** 0.47 | **Max DD:** 22.19% | **Trades:** 40 | **Win Rate:** 47.5%
- **Logic:** Trend-following breakout — enters when price closes above the BB Upper, confirming an actual breakout rather than a mean-reversion entry. Requires ATR rising, historical volatility rising, and positive 20-day ROC to confirm genuine momentum. Exits on BB Middle cross or MACD bearish cross.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - `Close > BB Upper`
  - `ATR(14) rising`
  - `Historical Vol(20) rising`
  - `ROC(20) > 0`
- **Exit conditions:**
  - `Close < BB Middle` (trend exhausted)
  - `MACD line < MACD signal` (momentum reversal)
- **Why it works for BA.L:** During BA.L's strong trending periods (2022–2025), this strategy captured the sustained upward moves. The ATR + HV rising filters prevent entering on one-off spikes. 40 trades provides reasonable statistical coverage.
- **Caveat:** 47.5% win rate is near coin-flip — returns driven by large winner trades. Lower max DD (22.19%) than bb_meanrev_upper.

### 3. hmm_regime — HMM Regime
- **Total Return:** 44.27% | **Sharpe:** 0.38 | **Max DD:** 21.45% | **Trades:** 12 | **Win Rate:** 50.0%
- **Logic:** Uses a 3-state HMM (Hidden Markov Model) to detect market regimes. Enters when bull regime probability > 60% AND trend alignment (EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200). Exits on EMA20 crossing below EMA50.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - `HMM bull prob > 60%`
  - `EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200`
- **Exit conditions:**
  - `EMA20 < EMA50`
- **Why it works for BA.L:** HMM regime detection successfully identified BA.L's major bullish periods (2016–2017, 2019–2020, 2021–2025) while avoiding the worst drawdown periods. Only 12 trades but each captures a major regime shift.
- **Caveat:** Very few trades (12). Requires HMM model to be running (regime data available in the dataset). 50% win rate but good risk-adjusted returns.

### 4. bb_meanrev_entry2 — BB MeanRev Entry2 (Config 2)
- **Total Return:** 30.24% | **Sharpe:** 0.47 | **Max DD:** 21.05% | **Trades:** 6 | **Win Rate:** 66.7%
- **Logic:** Same core signals as bb_meanrev (BB Lower + RSI<30 + W%R<-80) with two additional filters: Bollinger Band Width >= 10% (ensures the band has expanded sufficiently to indicate meaningful volatility) and ATR(14) >= 15 (ensures sufficient absolute price movement). Exit at BB Middle Band.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - `Close <= BB Lower`
  - `RSI(14) < 30`
  - `Williams %R < -80`
  - `BB Width >= 10%`
  - `ATR(14) >= 15`
- **Exit conditions:**
  - `Close >= BB Middle`
- **Why it works for BA.L:** The extra filters produce very few but high-quality signals (only 6 trades, 66.7% win rate). The BB Width filter ensures entries during genuinely volatile pullbacks, not narrow consolidation ranges. Profit Factor of 9.539 is exceptional.
- **Caveat:** Only 6 trades in 10 years — far too few for statistical significance. Excellent Profit Factor suggests high average win size relative to losses.

### 5. ema_pullback — EMA Pullback
- **Total Return:** 26.68% | **Sharpe:** 0.31 | **Max DD:** 21.86% | **Trades:** 59 | **Win Rate:** 30.5%
- **Logic:** Trend-following pullback entry — requires a clear uptrend (EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200) with price pulling back to within 3% of EMA20, RSI between 40–55 (not overbought, not oversold), and SuperTrend in bullish mode. Exits on EMA50 cross or RSI > 70.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - `EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200` (strong uptrend)
  - `|Close - EMA20| / Close < 3%` (pullback to EMA20)
  - `RSI(14) between 40 and 55` (moderate momentum)
  - `SuperTrend direction == 1` (bullish)
- **Exit conditions:**
  - `Close < EMA50` (trend broken)
  - `RSI(14) > 70` (overbought)
- **Why it works for BA.L:** BA.L's 2022–2025 bull run provided many pullback-to-EMA20 opportunities. The 59 trades give the best statistical coverage among profitable strategies. However, 30.5% win rate means most trades are small losses chopped through, with large occasional winners.
- **Caveat:** Low win rate (30.5%) but positive expectancy due to good risk/reward on winners. Underperforms in ranging markets.

## Phase 4: Custom Strategies

Four custom strategies exist for BA.L, all based on mean-reversion with various enhancements. None outperform the best built-in strategies, but they provide useful risk-adjusted alternatives.

### ba_defense_meanrev
- **Logic:** BB Lower + RSI<30 + W%R<-80 entry (same as bb_meanrev) with two volume confirmation filters: `CMF > 0` and `OBV rising`. Exit at BB Middle.
- **Results:** 0.00% return, 0 trades — the volume confirmation filters are too restrictive for BA.L's trading pattern. The defense sector has lower retail volume participation, so OBV/CMF filters miss legitimate mean-reversion setups.
- **Analysis:** Volume confirmation works well for liquid consumer stocks (LSEG, SVT) but not for defense stocks where institutional flow doesn't show up clearly in volume-based indicators.

### ba_regime_filter_meanrev
- **Logic:** BB Lower + RSI<30 + W%R<-80 entry, but only when HMM is not in bear regime (`bear_prob < 0.5`). Uses CMF > -0.1 (more lenient than ba_defense).
- **Results:** 2.66% return, 3 trades, 66.7% win rate. Positive but marginal — the regime filter helps avoid catastrophic entries during bear markets but produces very few signals.
- **Improvement over built-ins:** The regime awareness is sound for defense stocks that can trend lower during contract cancellations.

### ba_atr_trail_meanrev
- **Logic:** Standard BB Lower + RSI<30 + W%R<-80 entry with dual exit: BB Middle OR 3xATR trailing stop from 3-day high.
- **Results:** 5.93% return, 13 trades, 53.8% win rate, 1.319 profit factor. The ATR trail captures more upside during strong bounces but adds 31.07% max drawdown.
- **Improvement over built-ins:** Better than ba_defense_meanrev in terms of trade count but still underperforms bb_meanrev_upper. The ATR trail is valuable — a refined version with higher ATR multiplier could be worth testing.

### ba_multi_score_meanrev
- **Logic:** Weighted scoring system (volume 20pts, momentum 20pts, structure 20pts, MACD 15pts, CCI 10pts). Entry when score >= 60 AND BB Lower touch. Exit at BB Middle.
- **Results:** -2.99% return, 6 trades, 66.7% win rate but losing trades outweigh winners. The multi-score approach creates too many false entries — a score of 60 is not restrictive enough for this low-volume stock.
- **Improvement over built-ins:** Conceptually sound multi-factor approach but the scoring thresholds need adjustment for BA.L's characteristics.

## Phase 6: Final Recommendation

### Best Overall: `bb_meanrev_upper` (56.89% return, 0.51 Sharpe, 31.93% drawdown)
The combination of tight oversold entry conditions with a wide upper-band exit captures BA.L's full oscillation range. For a defense stock with ~12% Bollinger Band width, this is the most efficient use of the asset's natural volatility.

### Highest Return: `bb_meanrev_upper` (56.89%)
Same as above — the wider exit band provides 2× the mean-reversion capture compared to the standard BB Middle exit.

### Lowest Drawdown: `ema_pullback_vol` (17.11%)
The volatility-filtered EMA pullback strategy has the tightest drawdown but lower absolute returns (13.63%). Suitable for conservative allocation.

### Recommended Strategy: `bb_meanrev_upper`
- **Rationale:** Highest Sharpe ratio (0.51) among profitable strategies with meaningful returns. The 84.6% win rate is exceptional.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - `Close <= ta_bband_lower` (price at lower Bollinger Band)
  - `ta_rsi14 < 30` (RSI deeply oversold)
  - `ta_williams_r < -80` (Williams %R confirms extreme sell-off)
- **Exit conditions:**
  - `Close >= ta_bband_upper` (price reaches upper Bollinger Band)
- **Risk management:**
  - Position size: 2% of portfolio per trade (max £2,000 risk)
  - Given ~13 trades per 10 years, this is a **low-frequency, high-conviction** strategy
  - Consider a secondary time-stop: exit if price has not reached BB middle within 30 days (avoid capital lockup during extended downturns)
- **When it fails:**
  - **Strong trending markets** (2022–2025 bull run): Price may spend extended periods above BB Middle, never touching the lower band
  - **Gap-down events:** Defense stocks can gap down on contract cancellations, bypassing BB Lower entirely
  - **Low-volatility consolidation:** BB bands narrow during consolidation, making BB Lower touch rare even during pullbacks

### When to avoid / use alternatives:
- During BA.L's strong uptrend phases (EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200), consider `bb_breakout` (50.58% return, 22.19% max DD) to capture momentum
- During bearish regime periods (HMM bear prob > 50%), use `hmm_regime` (44.27% return) to trade only the strongest trend periods
- In choppy/ranging markets, `bb_meanrev` (standard BB Middle exit) provides more frequent signals with moderate returns
