## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. BB Breakout (bb_breakout) — Best Overall
- **Logic:** Enters when price breaks above the upper Bollinger Band, with confirmation that ATR is rising (volatility expanding), historical volatility is rising, and rate-of-change (ROC) is positive. Exits when price drops below the middle BB band or MACD turns bearish.
- **Entry conditions:** Close > BB Upper AND ATR rising AND HV rising AND ROC(20) > 0
- **Exit conditions:** Close < BB Middle OR MACD line < MACD signal
- **Results:** 26.43% return, 1.47 Sharpe, 5.24% MaxDD, 75% win rate, 4 trades
- **Trade details:**
  - 2025-07-18 → 2025-07-22: -4.54% (loss — early breakout fakeout)
  - 2025-09-10 → 2025-10-07: +8.34% (win)
  - 2026-01-02 → 2026-01-23: +15.12% (win — strong momentum breakout)
  - 2026-02-18 → 2026-03-20: +6.22% (win)
- **Why it worked:** BAE experienced 3 strong momentum bursts during the period, and the BB Breakout captured all 3 with a volatility filter (ATR rising + HV rising) that avoids false breakouts during low-volatility consolidation. The first trade was a loss, but the subsequent 3 wins were large enough to deliver excellent risk-adjusted returns.

### 2. BB MeanRev (Upper Exit) (bb_meanrev_upper)
- **Logic:** Classic mean-reversion: buy when price hits the lower BB band with RSI < 30 and Williams %R < -80. Exit at the upper BB band (full band capture).
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower AND RSI(14) < 30 AND Williams %R < -80
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Upper
- **Results:** 12.56% return, 1.21 Sharpe, 8.29% MaxDD, 100% win rate, 2 trades
- **Trade details:**
  - 2025-11-19 → 2026-01-02: +1.31% (small win)
  - 2026-10-07 → 2026-10-27: +11.12% (large win)
- **Why it worked:** The upper BB exit is more ambitious than the middle-band exit, allowing the strategy to capture the full width of mean-reversion cycles. The second trade alone (11.12%) was responsible for most of the return.

### 3. BB MeanRev Entry2 (bb_meanrev_entry2)
- **Logic:** Same entry as bb_meanrev but with additional quality filters: Bollinger Band Width ≥ 10% and ATR ≥ 15. These filters ensure trades only occur during high-volatility, wide-band conditions. Exits at BB Middle.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower AND RSI(14) < 30 AND Williams %R < -80 AND BB Width ≥ 10% AND ATR(14) ≥ 15
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Middle
- **Results:** 8.61% return, 1.14 Sharpe, 6.23% MaxDD, 100% win rate, 2 trades
- **Why it worked:** The quality filters reduced noise trades, but the wider exit target (middle band instead of upper band) meant smaller per-trade returns. The ATR ≥ 15 filter was important — BAE's ATR ranged from roughly 15–35 during the period.

### 4. BB Mean Reversion (bb_meanrev)
- **Logic:** Classic mean-reversion with BB Lower entry, RSI < 30, Williams %R < -80. Exits at BB Middle band.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower AND RSI(14) < 30 AND Williams %R < -80
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Middle
- **Results:** 7.88% return, 0.98 Sharpe, 8.29% MaxDD, 50% win rate, 2 trades
- **Trade details:**
  - 2025-11-19 → 2025-12-09: -0.28% (slight loss)
  - 2026-10-07 → 2026-10-16: +8.20% (large win)
- **Why it worked:** The classic BB mean-reversion signal caught 2 deep pullbacks. One was a small loss (the stock dipped slightly further before recovering), but the October 2026 pullback was a large 8.2% bounce.

### 5. BB MeanRev Optimized (bb_meanrev_optimized)

## Phase 5: Parameter Optimization

Given that BB Breakout was clearly the best strategy, the key optimization question is: could tighter or looser volatility filters improve its performance? The current signal requires 4 conditions simultaneously (Close > BB Upper + ATR rising + HV rising + ROC > 0). Potential experiments:
- **Relax HV filter:** Remove the historical volatility rising condition — would increase trade frequency but risk more false breakouts.
- **Looser ROC threshold:** ROC > -1 instead of ROC > 0 — enter earlier in the breakout.
- **Trailing stop:** Add an ATR-based trailing stop instead of exiting at the BB middle — could capture more of the upside in strong trends.

With only 4 trades and 16 months of data, any parameter optimization would risk overfitting. These experiments are recommended for future analysis with more data.

## Phase 6: Final Recommendation

- **Best overall:** BB Breakout (bb_breakout) — highest return (26.43%), best Sharpe (1.47), and lowest drawdown (5.24%) of any strategy.
- **Highest return:** BB Breakout — 26.43% total return.
- **Lowest drawdown:** BB Breakout — 5.24% max drawdown.
- **Conservative alternative:** BB MeanRev Upper Exit — 12.56% return, 1.21 Sharpe, 100% win rate on 2 trades.

### Entry rules (BB Breakout):
1. Close price must exceed the upper Bollinger Band (20-period, 2 standard deviations)
2. ATR(14) must be rising (today > yesterday) — confirming expanding volatility
3. Historical volatility(20) must be rising — confirming momentum building
4. ROC(20) must be positive — confirming the breakout direction is upward

### Exit rules:
1. Close drops below the middle Bollinger Band (20-period SMA)
2. MACD line crosses below MACD signal line (bearish momentum shift)

### Risk management:
- Full capital allocation (100% of portfolio per trade)
- Commission: 0.1%, Slippage: 0.1%
- No explicit stop-loss — the volatility filters (ATR + HV rising) serve as implicit risk management by avoiding low-momentum breakouts
- Average trade duration: ~10–30 days (all 4 trades closed within 1 month)

### When it fails:
- Extended sideways/choppy markets where the stock grinds near the BB Upper but doesn't have the momentum to sustain a breakout. The ATR + HV filters help but can't prevent all false breakouts (the first trade was a -4.5% loss).
- Sharp V-shaped reversals where the stock spikes above the BB Upper intraday but closes back inside — the signal triggers on close, so it may catch intraday spikes.
- Periods where BAE experiences sustained downtrends — the strategy has no short-side component.

### Suggested improvements:
- Add a position-sizing rule based on ATR (reduce size when ATR is high to compensate for wider swings)
- Implement a max-holding-period stop (e.g., 30 days) to avoid capital lockup
- Consider adding volume confirmation (e.g., CMF > 0) to filter out low-volume breakouts
- Backtest with a trailing ATR stop as an alternative exit alongside the BB Middle exit

### Classification
BAE Systems exhibits **volatile, range-bound behaviour with occasional momentum bursts**. The stock does not show a clear sustained trend, making pure trend-following strategies (Golden Cross, EMA Pullback) unprofitable (-18% to -21% returns). Bollinger Band strategies work well because: (1) the stock frequently touches lower bands during pullbacks, and (2) it occasionally breaks out with sustained momentum. As a defence contractor, BAE's fundamentals are relatively stable but its stock price responds to geopolitical events and procurement announcements, creating both mean-reversion opportunities and short-term momentum moves.
