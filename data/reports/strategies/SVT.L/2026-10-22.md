### svt_range_reversion_v2 — Best Custom Strategy (35.72% return)

**Logic:** Dual-path oversold entry with dual exit (BB middle + ATR trail).

**Entry conditions (OR of two paths):**
- **Path A:** Close ≤ BB Lower Band **AND** RSI(14) < 35 (classic mean-reversion)
- **Path B:** RSI(14) < 28 **AND** Williams %R < -85 **AND** CMF > -0.05 (extreme oversold with minimal selling)

**Exit conditions (OR of two exits):**
- Close ≥ BB Middle Band (quick reversion profit)
- Close < 5-day high − 3×ATR(14) (trailing stop protects gains)

**Results:** 35.72% total return, 0.45 Sharpe, 26.64% max DD, **1.75 profit factor**, 73.1% win rate (19 wins, 7 losses), 26 trades

**Analysis:** The **best custom strategy overall** with the highest total return (35.72%) and best profit factor (1.75). ### Rationale

1. **Fits SVT.L's asset profile:** As a range-bound utility with mean-reversion tendencies, the stock reliably bounces off BB Lower bands when fundamentally cheap.

2. **Fundamental quality gate:** The P/E filter ensures entries only occur when the stock is trading below its historical average multiple (41.88), reducing the risk of catching fundamentally-weak selloffs.

3. **Low turnover:** 8 trades in 10 years (~0.8 trades/year) means minimal transaction costs and tax drag. This is a "set and check quarterly" approach.

4. **Ultra-low drawdown:** At 3.03%, the strategy rarely loses more than 3% of portfolio value at any point — significantly better than all built-in strategies.

5. **100% win rate:** Every trade in the 10-year backtest was profitable. This reflects the dual-filter approach: both technical oversold (BB Lower + RSI) AND fundamental value (P/E < 45) must align.

### Practical Trading Guidelines

- **Entry checklist:**
  1. Check quarterly P/E is < 45 (from `data/pe-data/`)
  2. Wait for daily close below BB Lower (20, 2)
  3. Confirm RSI(14) < 45
  4. Confirm CMF > -0.1 (no capitulation selling)
  5. Enter on next open

- **Exit rules:**
  - Take profit at BB Middle Band (quick reversion)
  - Trail with 3×ATR if price keeps rising (protect gains)
  - Stop loss: Not explicitly used — the entry filters are designed to avoid losses

- **Position sizing:** Full allocation per trade (no partial sizing needed given the low trade frequency and high confidence of each signal)

- **Monitoring frequency:** Check signals once per week; verify P/E quarterly

### Limitations & Risk Considerations

1. **Limited sample size:** 8 trades in 10 years is statistically thin. A few outlier losses could significantly change the profile.

2. **P/E data latency:** P/E is updated quarterly. The signal may be based on stale fundamentals if the latest P/E report hasn't been published.

3. **Structural break risk:** If SVT.L's business model changes (e.g., regulatory cap changes, interest rate environment shift), the historical P/E mean may shift.

4. **No trend protection:** The strategy doesn't account for secular downtrends. In a prolonged bear market, even cheap utility stocks can keep falling.

5. **Capacity:** With £100k per trade, this strategy may not scale well for larger portfolios without significant slippage.

### Buy-and-Hold Benchmark

Over the 10-year period, SVT.L's buy-and-hold return likely exceeded all strategies due to the dividend reinvestment effect. The mean-reversion strategies aim to outperform through tactical entries rather than passive accumulation. For an income-focused investor, the 3.96% dividend yield plus price appreciation may be sufficient without active trading.

**Final verdict:** The P/E Value Reversion strategy is recommended for patient, value-oriented traders who prefer infrequent, high-conviction trades. It is NOT suitable for active traders seeking frequent signals, or for investors in strong bull markets where buy-and-hold likely outperforms. For most SVT.L holders, the dividend yield alone (3.96%) provides compelling income; the strategy adds tactical alpha on top.
