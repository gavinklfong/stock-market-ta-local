## Phase 3: Top Strategy Source Analysis

### 1. `trend_momentum` (Best overall: +12.23%, Sharpe 0.55)
**Logic:** Combines EMA trend alignment (EMA20 > EMA50) with momentum confirmation (RSI > 50, MACD bullish). Entry on pullbacks to EMA20 with volume support. Exit on EMA cross or ATR trailing stop.

**Strengths:** Simple, clean logic with few conditions — high signal frequency (10 trades). Captures the 2023 bull run well.

**Weaknesses:** -3.07% in 2025; only 10 trades over 4 years (low sample size).

### 2. `momentum_comp` (Second: +10.54%, Sharpe 0.36)
**Logic:** Multi-factor momentum: ROC(20) + MACD histogram + RSI slope. Uses a composite momentum score. Entry when score > threshold; exit when momentum deteriorates.

**Strengths:** Best 2024 performer (+9.29%). 16 trades provides better statistical basis.

**Weaknesses:** 17.88% max DD; 2026 loss (-4.02%).

### 3. `ema_pullback_vol` (Third: +7.47%, Sharpe 0.43)
**Logic:** EMA alignment + pullback to EMA20 + Bollinger Band volatility filter + RSI range. More conservative than `trend_momentum`.

**Strengths:** Good risk-adjusted returns (0.43 Sharpe). Volatility filter reduces false entries. 47.1% win rate.

**Weaknesses:** Lower total return than top 2.

### 4. `regime_adaptive` (Sharpe 0.55, but 1 trade only)
**Logic:** Uses HMM regime probabilities to filter entries — only enters during bull regime with highest confidence. Very selective.

**Strengths:** Highest Sharpe ratio (0.55, tied with trend_momentum), lowest DD (0.56%).

**Weaknesses:** Only 1 trade — not statistically meaningful.

### 5. `vwap` (Most trades: 38, but high DD)
**Logic:** Simple VWAP trend-following. Entry when price > rising VWAP + RSI > 50. Exit on VWAP cross.

**Strengths:** Highest trade frequency (38 trades). Captures strong trends well.

**Weaknesses:** 29.1% max drawdown — dangerous risk profile.

### New Custom Strategy: `lseg_trend_guard`

**Design rationale:** The core weakness of all top strategies in Phase 2 was 2025 performance (-3% to -4%). The `lseg_trend_guard` strategy was specifically designed to address this by:
1. Using tighter ATR trailing stops (2.5x) to exit faster during crashes
2. Adding a time-stop concept (30-day hold limit)
3. Wider pullback entry (price near EMA20) for better risk-reward
4. Multiple confirmation filters to avoid entering during uncertain regimes

**Entry conditions (ALL must hold):**
- EMA alignment: EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200
- Pullback entry: Price within 3% below EMA20
- RSI 40-70: Trending but not overbought
- MACD bullish: MACD line > signal
- Volume confirmation: CMF > 0

**Exit conditions (ANY triggers exit):**
- EMA20 crosses below EMA50 (trend broken)
- ATR trailing stop: Price drops 2.5xATR below 15-day high
- RSI > 70 (overbought exit)

## Phase 5: Parameter Optimization

Four parameter configurations were tested for the trend_guard strategy:

| Config | ATR Mult | RSI Upper | Pullback % | Return | Sharpe | Max DD | Trades |
|--------|----------|-----------|------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **opt1** | 2.0x | 70 | 2% | +4.19% | 0.53 | **3.82%** | 3 |
| opt2 | 2.5x | 75 | 3% | +4.20% | 0.53 | 4.60% | 3 |
| opt4 | 3.0x | 75 | 3% | +4.07% | 0.51 | 4.73% | 3 |
| opt3 | 1.5x | 70 | 1% | +1.92% | 0.33 | 2.48% | 3 |

**Optimization findings:**
- The tighter ATR stop (opt1, 2.0x) gives the lowest drawdown (3.82%) with near-identical returns to baseline
- Wider pullback (3%) doesn't significantly increase trade count — the core issue is the AND-logic filter stack, not individual parameters
- Tightest stop (opt3, 1.5x) reduces returns — too sensitive to noise
- **Recommendation:** opt1 (ATR 2.0x, RSI < 70, 2% pullback) for risk-averse applications

**Fundamental limitation:** All configurations produce exactly 3 trades. The strategy's core design (5 AND conditions) is inherently too restrictive for LSEG.L. This is not a parameter problem but an architecture problem.

### Key Insights

1. **2025 was the dominant year** — all strategies lost money; the difference was magnitude (-3% to -4.14% for top performers)
2. **No strategy was perfectly crash-proof** — only the newly developed trend_guard had zero exposure, but at the cost of missing bull years

4. **LSEG_L_vwap_regime is the best custom strategy** — it captures the upside of bull years (13.57% in 2023) while keeping DD manageable (7.83%)
5. **The trend_guard approach validates conceptually** but needs fewer entry filters or a different entry mechanism to achieve viable trade counts

### Recommended Approach: Dual-Strategy Portfolio

For LSEG.L, a combination approach is recommended:

- **Primary (70% allocation): `trend_momentum`** — Highest total return (12.23%), clean trend-following logic
- **Risk overlay (30% allocation): `LSEG_L_vwap_regime`** — Complementary VWAP-based approach with regime filtering

**Expected combined profile:**
- Return: ~10-11% (weighted average)
- Sharpe: ~0.48-0.50
- Max DD: ~8-10% (diversification benefit)

### If Capital Preservation is Priority

Use `lseg_trend_guard_opt1` as a satellite strategy:
- It completely avoided the 2025 drawdown
- Only 3 trades over 4 years means it won't capture much upside
- Use it alongside `trend_momentum` to reduce overall portfolio DD during crash years

### Limitations

- Only 4 years of analysis data (2023-2026); longer backtest would provide more statistical power
- LSEG.L's extreme P/E (187.68) suggests the stock may be in a valuation bubble — consider fundamental risk
- The 2025 crash (-21.95%) may be a unique event; future crashes may behave differently
- Low trade counts (3-10 per strategy) mean results are not statistically robust
