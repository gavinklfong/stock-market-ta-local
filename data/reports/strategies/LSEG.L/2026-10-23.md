## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. VWAP Trend (vwap) — Best Return
**Logic:** Enter when Close > VWAP, VWAP rising, and RSI > 50. Exit on Close < VWAP.

**Entry conditions:**
- Close price above VWAP (20-day volume-weighted average price)
- VWAP trending upward (VWAP today > VWAP yesterday)
- RSI(14) > 50 (momentum confirms uptrend)

**Exit conditions:**
- Close falls below VWAP (trend reversal signal)

**Results:** Return=93.78%, Sharpe=0.61, MaxDD=29.10%, Trades=93, WinRate=40.2%, PF=1.40

**Analysis:** This is the simplest and most effective strategy for LSEG.L. The VWAP acts as a dynamic support in strong trends. LSEG.L's characteristics — high institutional ownership, financial services sector with strong trend persistence — make it ideal for VWAP-based trend following. The 40.2% win rate is low but each winning trade captures significant moves. The 29.1% drawdown is the main weakness.

**Signal frequency:** 49% of days satisfy entry conditions, but distinct trade clusters are only 125.

### 2. Momentum Composite (momentum_comp) — Best Risk-Adjusted Non-Custom
**Logic:** Multi-factor momentum entry. RSI > 55, MACD bullish, ROC > 0, Williams %R > -50, CMF > 0, EMA20 > EMA50.

**Entry conditions:**
- RSI(14) > 55
- MACD line > MACD signal
- ROC(20) > 0
- Williams %R > -50
- CMF > 0 (volume confirms)
- EMA20 > EMA50 (trend alignment)

**Exit conditions:**
- RSI < 50 AND MACD < Signal (both momentum indicators flip)

**Results:** Return=63.44%, Sharpe=0.52, MaxDD=18.52%, Trades=45, WinRate=43.2%, PF=1.45

**Analysis:** More conservative than VWAP but still strong. The multi-factor entry reduces false signals. Lower drawdown (18.52% vs 29.10%) makes this a solid secondary choice. However, it has lower total return because it misses some extended trends.

### 3. Trend+Momentum Combo (trend_momentum)
**Logic:** Requires BOTH golden cross conditions AND momentum composite conditions. Essentially, trend AND momentum must align.

**Entry conditions:**
- Golden Cross: EMA20>EMA50>EMA200, MA20 slope>0, SuperTrend=+1, RSI>50, MACD>Signal
- Momentum: RSI>55, MACD>Signal, ROC>0, Williams%R>-50, CMF>0

**Exit conditions:**
- EMA20 < EMA50 OR RSI < 50

**Results:** Return=48.68%, Sharpe=0.47, MaxDD=15.92%, Trades=34, WinRate=38.2%, PF=1.71

**Analysis:** The strictest entry conditions produce the lowest drawdown among trending strategies (15.92%). However, the lower return means the extra conservatism doesn't pay off in terms of risk-adjusted returns. The high profit factor (1.71) indicates each winning trade is well above average.

### 4. BB MeanRev (ATR Trail) (bb_meanrev_atr)
**Logic:** Mean reversion from BB Lower with ATR-based trailing stop. RSI < 35, Close <= BB Lower. Exit at BB Middle or 4xATR trail.

**Entry conditions:**
- Close <= Bollinger Band Lower
- RSI(14) < 35

**Exit conditions:**
- Close >= BB Middle, OR Close < (highest 5-day - 4xATR)

**Results:** Return=46.10%, Sharpe=0.42, MaxDD=29.86%, Trades=31, WinRate=67.7%, PF=1.75

**Analysis:** Surprisingly, mean reversion works moderately well for LSEG.L. The 67.7% win rate is excellent, but the 4xATR stop is wide, so wins are small and losses can be large. The PF of 1.75 is the highest among the top 5 — meaning profit per winning trade significantly exceeds loss per losing trade. However, the strategy underperforms trend strategies overall, suggesting LSEG.L is more trending than mean-reverting.

### 5. EMA Pullback + Vol (ema_pullback_vol)
**Logic:** Uptrend pullback to EMA20 with volume confirmation. RSI 35-55 near EMA20, SuperTrend=+1, rising volume.

**Entry conditions:**
- EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 (strong uptrend)
- Price within 3% of EMA20 (pullback)
- RSI(14) between 35 and 55
- SuperTrend = +1
- Volume > 80% of 10-day average

**Exit conditions:**
- Close < EMA50 OR RSI > 65

**Results:** Return=37.82%, Sharpe=0.40, MaxDD=27.36%, Trades=55, WinRate=47.3%, PF=1.29

**Analysis:** The weakest of the top 5. The high trade count (55) and low win rate (47.3%) suggest it's chasing pullbacks that don't always bounce. The strategy is too sensitive — price oscillates near EMA20 frequently, generating many marginal entries.

## Phase 4: Custom Strategies

### LSEG.L VWAP + Regime Filter — **BEST CUSTOM STRATEGY**
**Logic:** Combines the best of VWAP Trend with HMM regime filtering and ATR trailing stops. Designed specifically for LSEG.L's strong trending behavior with clear regime structure.

**Entry rules:**
- Close > VWAP (trend confirmed)
- VWAP rising (momentum building)
- RSI(14) between 50 and 65 (trending but not overbought)
- CMF > 0 (volume confirms buying pressure)
- Bull regime (HMM regime probability for state 0 >= state 1 and state 2)
- EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 (full trend alignment)

**Exit rules:**
- Close < VWAP (trend broken)
- ATR trailing stop hit: Close < (highest 10-day close - 3xATR)
- Regime shifts to bear (HMM state 1 probability > state 0)

**Results:** Return=57.11%, Sharpe=**0.66**, MaxDD=**12.98%**, Trades=49, WinRate=44.9%, PF=**1.985**

**Improvements over built-in VWAP:**
- **Sharpe ratio:** 0.66 vs 0.61 (+8.2%)
- **Max drawdown:** 12.98% vs 29.10% (**55.4% reduction**)
- **Profit factor:** 1.985 vs 1.40 (+41.8%)
- **Same return:** 57.11% vs 93.78% (lower raw return, but dramatically better risk profile)

**Why it works:** The HMM regime filter is the key innovation. It prevents entering during bear regimes when VWAP trends can be false signals. The ATR trailing stop (which rarely fires — regime shift exits are dominant) provides additional protection. The RSI cap at 65 avoids chasing overbought tops.

### LSEG.L Trend Score
**Logic:** Multi-factor scoring system combining VWAP, EMA, MACD, volume, and regime into a single score (0-100). Entry at score >= 50, exit at score < 30.

**Results:** Return=35.18%, Sharpe=0.30, MaxDD=50.15%, Trades=73, WinRate=27.8%, PF=1.108

**Analysis:** The scoring approach is too aggressive — entering at score >= 50 generates too many marginal signals. The 73 trades and 27.8% win rate show that many scores are driven by single indicators. The 50.15% drawdown is unacceptable. This approach needs a higher threshold (>= 70) to be viable.

### LSEG.L VWAP + ATR Exit
**Logic:** Identical entry to vanilla VWAP Trend, but adds ATR trailing stop as alternative exit alongside VWAP cross.

**Results:** Return=95.47%, Sharpe=0.62, MaxDD=29.10%, Trades=93, WinRate=40.2%, PF=1.408

**Analysis:** Essentially identical to vanilla VWAP. The ATR trailing stop doesn't fire in most cases — the VWAP cross exit is dominant. This confirms that for LSEG.L, VWAP cross is the primary exit mechanism.

### Lowest Drawdown: **LSEG.L VWAP + Regime Filter**
- **Max Drawdown:** 12.98% (vs. 29.10% for VWAP, 15.92% for Trend+Momentum)

### Recommended Entry Rules
1. **Primary:** Close > 20-day VWAP AND VWAP is rising
2. **Momentum:** RSI(14) between 50 and 65
3. **Volume:** CMF > 0 (confirmed buying pressure)
4. **Regime:** HMM state 0 (bull) probability is the highest of the three states
5. **Trend:** EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 (full bullish alignment)
6. **ALL conditions must be met simultaneously**

### Recommended Exit Rules
1. **Primary:** Close falls below VWAP (trend reversal)
2. **Regime shift:** HMM bear state (state 1) becomes dominant
3. **ATR stop:** (optional, rarely triggers) 3xATR trail below 10-day high

### Risk Management
- **Position sizing:** Risk no more than 2% of portfolio per trade
- **Stop loss:** 3xATR below entry (for initial stop, though regime shift handles this)
- **Max holding period:** No explicit time stop — exit on signal reversal
- **Weekday considerations:** Tuesday entries preferred (lower slippage); avoid Friday entries (weekend gap risk)

### When This Strategy Fails
1. **Bear market regimes:** The regime filter prevents entries, so the strategy sits flat (14.12% total in 10 years of mostly flat periods)
2. **Volatile chop:** If HMM regime doesn't clearly favor bull vs bear, no entries are generated
3. **VWAP trend traps:** In sideways markets, VWAP can trend while price mean-reverts around it (mitigated by EMA alignment filter)
4. **Regime lag:** HMM regime detection has some lag — by the time bear regime is detected, some loss has already occurred
5. **2025 performance:** The strategy lost -2.7% in 2025, showing it's not immune to broad market declines
