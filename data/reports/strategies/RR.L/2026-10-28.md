## Executive Summary
- **Company:** Rolls-Royce Holdings (RR.L)
- **Sector:** Aerospace & Defence

- **Best Overall:** BB Breakout — 19.59% return, 1.44 Sharpe, 4.22% drawdown
- **Highest Return:** BB Breakout — 19.59% return

Rolls-Royce Holdings is a global aerospace leader designing cutting-edge power systems for aircraft and ships. As a defence/aerospace stock, it exhibits high volatility driven by engine delivery cycles, regulatory approvals, and macroeconomic conditions. With an annualized volatility of 41.34% — significantly higher than typical utility stocks — RR.L responds well to breakout and momentum strategies that capitalize on its large directional moves.

## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. BB Breakout — Best Overall
- **Logic:** Enter when price closes above Bollinger Band Upper, with rising ATR and rising historical volatility, and positive 20-day rate of change. Exit when price falls below BB middle or MACD crosses bearish.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - Close > Bollinger Band Upper (20, 2)
  - ATR(14) rising (current > previous)
  - Historical volatility rising
  - ROC(20) > 0 (positive momentum)
- **Exit conditions:**
  - Close < Bollinger Band Middle (20-period SMA), OR
  - MACD line crosses below signal line
- **Results:** 19.59% return, 1.44 Sharpe, 4.22% MaxDD, 15.18 PF, 66.7% win rate, 6 trades
- **Trade details:**
  - Trade 1: 2025-06-03 @ 895.09 → 2025-06-12 @ 886.51, -1.16% (stop hit)
  - Trade 2: 2025-06-26 @ 934.73 → 2025-07-21 @ 999.00, +6.67%
  - Trade 3: 2025-07-31 @ 1073.07 → 2025-08-15 @ 1072.93, -0.21% (slight loss)
  - Trade 4: 2025-09-11 @ 1124.62 → 2025-10-03 @ 1167.33, +3.59%
  - Trade 5: 2025-12-18 @ 1145.14 → 2026-01-21 @ 1253.74, +9.27% (largest winner)
  - Trade 6: 2026-08-03 @ 1508.31 → 2026-08-19 @ 1517.88, +0.43%
- **Analysis:** This strategy excelled because RR.L had several strong breakout rallies (Jun-Jul 2025, Sep-Oct 2025, Dec-Jan 2025-26) where price broke above BB Upper and sustained the move. The ATR/HRV filters ensured entries only during active volatility expansion. The MACD exit captured the bulk of trends while avoiding whipsaws in choppy periods. The exceptional profit factor of 15.18 reflects the asymmetry: 4 winners averaged +4.99% vs 2 losers averaging -0.69%.

### 2. BB MeanRev (Upper Exit)
- **Logic:** Entry at BB Lower + RSI < 30 + Williams %R < -80 (deep oversold). Exit when price reaches BB Upper.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - Close ≤ Bollinger Band Lower
  - RSI(14) < 30 (oversold)
  - Williams %R(14) < -80 (deeply oversold)
- **Exit conditions:**
  - Close ≥ Bollinger Band Upper (full band traversal)
- **Results:** 10.81% return, 0.92 Sharpe, 7.76% MaxDD, 100% win rate, 2 trades
- **Trade details:**
  - Trade 1: 2025-11-24 @ 1030.03 → 2025-12-18 @ 1142.86, +10.74%
  - Trade 2: 2026-10-22 @ 1260.86 → 2026-10-28 @ 1263.00, +0.07%
- **Analysis:** Only 2 signals in 16 months — the strict triple-oversold condition rarely triggers. Trade 1 was a textbook bounce from oversold to overbought (+10.74%), validating the mean-reversion logic for RR.L's volatile swings. Trade 2 barely reached the target. The low trade count is both a strength (high conviction signals) and weakness (missed opportunities).

### 3. Trend+Momentum Combo

### 4. EMA Pullback
- **Logic:** In a bullish trend (EMA20>EMA50>EMA200, SuperTrend bullish), buy when price pulls back within 3% of EMA20 and RSI is in the 40-55 range (not overbought). Exit when price falls below EMA50 or RSI > 70.
- **Entry conditions:**
  - EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 (bullish trend)
  - Price within 3% of EMA20 (pullback zone)
  - RSI(14) between 40 and 55 (moderate, not overbought)
  - SuperTrend direction = bullish
- **Exit conditions:**
  - Close < EMA50 (trend broken), OR
  - RSI(14) > 70 (overbought)
- **Results:** 9.29% return, 0.49 Sharpe, 25.38% MaxDD, 1.27 PF, 25% win rate, 12 trades
- **Trade details:** 12 trades, 3 winners (largest: +19.13%, +14.81%, +11.78%) and 9 losers. Notable loss: Trade 6 (-7.66%), Trade 7 (-6.88%) during March-April 2026 selloff.
- **Analysis:** The high trade count (12) reflects the looser entry conditions. Win rate is low at 25%, but the 3 big winners (+19%, +15%, +12%) more than offset the 9 small losses. The strategy is sensitive to RR.L's high volatility — many false pullbacks that don't recover.

### 5. MultiScore 60+
- **Logic:** Multi-factor scoring system with relaxed threshold (≥60 instead of 80). Scores trend (30pts), momentum (25pts), volume (20pts), and structure (5pts). Lower threshold triggers more signals.
- **Entry conditions:** Total score ≥ 60 from:
  - Trend: EMA20>EMA50 (10), EMA50>EMA200 (10), SuperTrend bullish (10)
  - Momentum: RSI>50 (8), MACD bullish (8), ROC>0 (5), Williams %R>-50 (4)
  - Volume: CMF>0 (10), OFI>0 (10)
  - Structure: BOS breakout (1)
- **Exit conditions:** Score drops below threshold
- **Results:** 8.38% return, 0.41 Sharpe, 26.05% MaxDD, 1.19 PF, 27.3% win rate, 11 trades
- **Trade details:**
  - Trade 1: 2025-06-02 @ 869.67 → 2025-10-13 @ 1127.37, +29.40% (best overall trade)
  - Trade 5: 2025-12-11 @ 1101.10 → 2026-01-28 @ 1206.79, +9.39%
  - Trade 10: 2026-06-12 @ 1309.31 → 2026-09-01 @ 1457.74, +11.13%
- **Analysis:** Trade 1 held for 4 months and captured +29.40% — the single best trade of any strategy. The low win rate (27.3%) mirrors EMA Pullback, but the few large winners carry the strategy. Trade 7 was a big loser (-11.40%) during the April 2026 correction, highlighting the risk of holding through sharp reversals.

## Phase 6: Final Recommendation

- **Best overall:** **BB Breakout** — 19.59% return with 1.44 Sharpe ratio and only 4.22% maximum drawdown. Exceptional profit factor of 15.18 means winners vastly outweigh losers.
- **Highest return (single trade):** MultiScore 60+ — captured a +29.40% move over 4 months in Trade 1
- **Lowest drawdown:** BB Breakout — 4.22% max drawdown, far superior to all other strategies

**Recommended entry rules (BB Breakout):**
1. Price closes above Bollinger Band Upper (20, 2)
2. ATR(14) is rising (current > previous bar)
3. Historical volatility(20) is rising
4. ROC(20) > 0 (positive momentum)

**Recommended exit rules:**
1. Price closes below Bollinger Band Middle (20-period SMA), OR
2. MACD line crosses below signal line (bearish cross)

**Risk management:**
- Position sizing: Full capital allocation per trade (consistent with backtest)
- Stop loss: Implicit via BB Middle cross or MACD bearish cross
- No additional hard stop — the BB Middle acts as a dynamic trailing exit
- Consider adding ATR(14) x 2 as an additional hard stop below entry for catastrophic protection

**When it fails:**
- **Choppy, low-volatility markets:** The ATR and HV rising filters will prevent entries, which is protective
- **Rapid V-shape reversals:** The MACD exit lags; price could reverse sharply before MACD turns bearish
- **Earnings/gap events:** RR.L is sensitive to company announcements; a gap above BB Upper could be followed by an immediate gap down

**Suggested improvements:**
1. Add a time-based stop: exit if no progress after 20 trading days
2. Replace fixed BB Middle exit with ATR-based trailing stop to capture larger trends
3. Filter entries by sector ETF performance (e.g., only enter when aerospace sector is in uptrend)
