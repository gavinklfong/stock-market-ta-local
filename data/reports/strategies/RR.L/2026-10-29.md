## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. `bb_breakout` — BB Breakout (RECOMMENDED)
- **Logic:** Enters when price breaks above the upper Bollinger Band with rising ATR, rising historical volatility, and positive ROC(20). Exits when price falls below the BB middle band or MACD crosses bearishly.
- **Entry conditions:** Close > BB Upper AND ATR rising AND historical volatility rising AND ROC(20) > 0
- **Exit conditions:** Close < BB Middle OR MACD line < MACD signal
- **Results:** 19.59% return, 1.44 Sharpe, 4.22% max DD, 15.18 profit factor, 66.7% win rate, 6 trades
- **Analysis:** This is the clear winner. RR.L's strong uptrend (2023-2025) produced frequent Bollinger Band breakouts. The rising ATR and HV filters ensured entries during genuine volatility expansions, not noise. The exit (BB middle or MACD cross) was well-timed to capture most of the move before pullbacks. With only 6 trades and a 15.18 profit factor, each winning trade was highly profitable. The Calmar ratio of 4.73 is exceptional.

### 2. `bb_meanrev_upper` — BB MeanRev (Upper Exit)
- **Logic:** Classic mean reversion — enters at BB Lower with RSI < 30 and Williams %R < -80, but exits at BB Upper (full band-to-band) instead of BB Middle.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower AND RSI(14) < 30 AND Williams %R < -80
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Upper
- **Results:** 12.30% return, 1.03 Sharpe, 7.76% max DD, 100% win rate, 2 trades
- **Analysis:** The key improvement over `bb_meanrev` is the upper-band exit target. Since RR.L was in a strong uptrend, mean-reversion dips were quickly bought back to new highs. Exiting at the upper band (instead of the middle) captured the full recovery. With only 2 trades, this is a small sample, but the 100% win rate is notable.

### 3. `trend_momentum` — Trend+Momentum Combo
- **Logic:** Requires BOTH a golden cross configuration (EMA20>EMA50>EMA200, MA20 slope>0, SuperTrend+1, RSI>50, MACD bullish) AND momentum confirmation (RSI>55, MACD>Signal, ROC20>0, Williams %R>-50, CMF>0). Both sets must be true simultaneously.
- **Entry conditions:** Golden cross conditions AND momentum conditions must both hold
- **Exit conditions:** EMA20 < EMA50 OR RSI < 50
- **Results:** 10.46% return, 0.57 Sharpe, 17.51% max DD, 1.82 profit factor, 66.7% win rate, 6 trades
- **Analysis:** The dual-requirement design filtered for high-quality setups. However, the 17.51% max drawdown was the second-worst among profitable strategies. The exit (EMA20 < EMA50 or RSI < 50) was too late to protect against the late-stage correction. This strategy would have performed better in the 2023-2024 period but was caught in the 2026 pullback.

### 4. `ema_pullback` — EMA Pullback
- **Logic:** Enters during pullbacks in an uptrend — requires EMA20>EMA50>EMA200, price within 3% of EMA20, RSI between 40-55, and SuperTrend positive. Exits at EMA50 breach or RSI > 70.
- **Entry conditions:** EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 AND |Close - EMA20|/Close < 3% AND RSI 40-55 AND SuperTrend+1
- **Exit conditions:** Close < EMA50 OR RSI > 70
- **Results:** 9.29% return, 0.49 Sharpe, 25.38% max DD, 1.27 profit factor, 25.0% win rate, 12 trades
- **Analysis:** High trade count with low win rate (3 of 12 winning). The 25.38% max drawdown nearly wiped out gains from earlier trades. The RSI > 70 exit was too tight for a strong-trend stock that often stayed overbought for extended periods. The strategy caught some good pullbacks but bled money on false signals.

### 5. `bb_meanrev` / `bb_meanrev_optimized` — BB Mean Reversion
- **Logic:** Classic mean reversion — enters at BB Lower with RSI < 30 and Williams %R < -80. Exits at BB Middle. The "optimized" and "entry2" variants are identical in signal logic.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower AND RSI(14) < 30 AND Williams %R < -80
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Middle
- **Results:** 7.10% return, 0.68 Sharpe, 7.76% max DD, 100% win rate, 2 trades
- **Analysis:** Same signals as `bb_meanrev_upper` but with a more conservative exit target (BB Middle instead of BB Upper). The 7.10% return is solid but significantly underperforms `bb_meanrev_upper` (12.30%). In a trending stock like RR.L, leaving money on the table by exiting early is a consistent pattern. The 2-trade sample size is small, but the 100% win rate confirms that dips were reliably bought in this period.

**Recommended custom strategy design for RR.L:**

Given the analysis, a custom strategy combining breakout confirmation with volatility filtering would be most promising:

- **Entry:** BB Breakout signal (Close > BB Upper + ATR rising + HV rising) + volume confirmation (CMF > 0) + regime filter (HMM bull probability > neutral)
- **Exit:** ATR trailing stop (3×ATR from 5-day high) OR MACD bearish cross (whichever comes first)
- **Rationale:** RR.L's breakout strategy worked well but with only 6 trades. Adding volume confirmation would reduce false breakouts, and an ATR trailing stop would capture more of the trend move compared to the conservative BB Middle exit.

## Phase 6: Final Recommendation

### Best Overall: `bb_breakout`
- **Return:** 19.59% | **Sharpe:** 1.44 | **Max DD:** 4.22% | **Calmar:** 4.73
- **Rationale:** Highest risk-adjusted return by a wide margin. The Calmar ratio of 4.73 means the strategy generated 4.73× more return than its worst drawdown. The 15.18 profit factor means each dollar lost was offset by $15 in gains. This is exceptional for a 5-month backtest period.

### Entry Rules
1. Close price must break above the upper Bollinger Band (20, 2)
2. ATR(14) must be rising (today > yesterday)
3. Historical volatility (20) must be rising
4. ROC(20) must be positive (price above 20-day lookback)

### Exit Rules
1. **Primary:** Close falls below Bollinger Band middle (20)
2. **Secondary:** MACD line crosses below MACD signal

### Risk Management
- **Position sizing:** Full capital allocation per trade (consistent with backtest)
- **Stop loss:** Not explicitly coded — the BB Middle / MACD cross exits serve as implicit stops
- **Holding period:** Variable (0–several days per trade); average duration was 0 days (likely same-day entries/exits due to signal nature)
- **Maximum exposure:** 1 trade at a time (signal-based, not portfolio-based)

### When This Strategy Fails
- **Choppy, range-bound markets:** The BB breakout will generate false signals during sideways action (e.g., if RR.L consolidates after a big move)
- **Gap-down openings:** The breakout entry may be filled at a worse price than the close, and the exit may gap through the BB middle
- **Low volatility environments:** When ATR is declining and HV is falling, breakout conditions won't trigger — the strategy will sit in cash
- **Continuation crashes:** In a sharp sell-off (like 2020), the BB Middle exit may come too late

### Suggested Improvements
1. **Add volume confirmation** (CMF > 0) to filter false breakouts
2. **Replace BB Middle exit with ATR trailing stop** to capture extended trends
3. **Add regime filter** (HMM) to avoid trading during bear regimes
4. **Add position sizing** based on ATR (risk 2% per trade)
5. **Backtest on longer period** (2016-2026) to validate across market cycles
