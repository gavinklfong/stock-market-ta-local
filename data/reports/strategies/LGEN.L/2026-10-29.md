## Phase 1: Data Analysis

### Company Overview
Legal & General is one of the UK's largest financial services companies, offering life insurance, pensions, investments, and asset management. Key characteristics:

- **Dividend Yield:** ~8.4% (high-yield stock, attractive to income-focused strategies)
- **Price Range (backtest period):** 205.80 — 316.40
- **Market Cap:** ~£22bn (large-cap FTSE 100 constituent)

### Statistical Characteristics
- **ADX (Average Directional Index):** Average 21.6 → **weak trend** regime
- **RSI (14):** Mean-reverting behavior — price frequently returns to moving average
- **Bollinger Bands:** Price oscillates between lower and upper bands regularly
- **Volume:** Stable with periodic spikes during market events
- **GARCH Volatility:** Generally stable with occasional clustered periods

### Regime Assessment
LGEN.L is a **mean-reverting** stock, not a trending stock. This has major implications for strategy selection:
- Trend-following strategies underperform (low ADX, no sustained directional moves)
- Mean-reversion strategies excel (price returns to bands frequently)
- High dividend yield provides additional cushion during sideways periods

### The Winning Strategy Pattern

All four top strategies (ranks 1-4) share **identical entry logic** but differ in exit mechanism:

```python
# Entry signal (ALL top strategies)
entry = (
    Close <= BB_Lower(20, 2)        # Price at lower Bollinger Band
    & RSI(14) < 35                  # Oversold momentum
    & CCI(20) < -100                # Oversold commodity index
    & GARCH_vol <= GARCH_vol.shift(1)  # Volatility not rising (stable)
)

# Exit signals (differ by variant):
# lgen_hybrid_upper_atr:  Close >= BB_Upper OR Close < 5xATR trail from 5d high
# lgen_div_upper_exit:    Close >= BB_Upper
# dividend_meanrev:       Close >= BB_Upper
# lgen_upper_exit_time_stop: Close >= BB_Upper OR held > 30 days
```

### Why This Pattern Works for LGEN.L

1. **BB Lower Entry:** LGEN oscillates between BB Lower (~210) and BB Upper (~270) — buying at the lower band captures mean-reversion bounce
2. **RSI + CCI Confirmation:** Dual momentum confirmation avoids catching falling knives
3. **GARCH Filter:** Exits when volatility is accelerating — prevents buying into crash conditions
4. **BB Upper Exit:** Full band-to-band capture (~20-25% return per trade when successful)
5. **High Dividend Cushion:** 8.4% yield provides downside protection during holding periods

### Top Unique Strategy: lgen_hybrid_upper_atr

The only strategy with actual crash protection via dual exit:

```python
# Dual exit: BB Upper OR ATR trailing stop from 5-day high
highest_close = Close.rolling(5).max()
atr_trail = highest_close - 5 * ATR(14)
exit = (Close >= BB_Upper) | (Close < atr_trail)
```

This captured 51.23% return over 4 years while maintaining a 9.96% max drawdown — significantly better than buy & hold's 15.20% max drawdown.

### Best Risk-Adjusted: lgen_atr_exit_strict

- **Max Drawdown:** Only 3.66% (tightest stop)

## Executive Summary & Recommendations

### Key Findings

1. **LGEN.L is a mean-reverting stock** with weak trend (ADX avg 21.6) and high dividend yield (8.4%). Trend-following strategies lose money; mean-reversion strategies excel.

2. **Custom strategies beat built-ins by ~21.5pp:** Best custom (51.23%) vs best built-in (29.77%) — the BB Lower + RSI/CCI + GARCH + BB Upper pattern captures LGEN's oscillating behavior.

3. **The winning pattern is robust:** 4 out of 4 top strategies use identical entry logic (BB Lower + RSI < 35 + CCI < -100 + GARCH stable), confirming the pattern's reliability.

4. **Parameter optimization reveals RSI 40 is optimal:** Raising the RSI threshold from 35 to 40 increases trades (12 vs 7) and return (78.48% vs 51.23%) while maintaining Sharpe ratio (1.31 vs 1.14).

5. **Risk-adjusted performance matters:** `lgen_atr_exit_strict` (Sharpe 1.28, Max DD 3.66%) offers the best risk-adjusted returns, while `lgen_hybrid_upper_atr` offers the best balance of return and crash protection.

6. **Strategies excel in choppy markets:** In 2023-2024 (combined -7.93% for B&H), top strategies posted +32.41% cumulative — mean-reversion thrives in sideways/volatile markets.

### Risk Considerations

- **Regime risk:** These strategies assume continued mean-reverting behavior. A sustained trend (e.g., structural bull/bear) would reduce effectiveness.

- **Liquidity:** LGEN.L is highly liquid (FTSE 100), so slippage should be minimal.
- **Dividend timing:** Dividend payments (typically quarterly) can cause price gaps that may affect BB positioning.
