### Sector-Appropriate Analysis
LondonMetric Property is a UK-listed property REIT, not a commodity or cyclical growth stock. Real estate equities are highly sensitive to interest rates (higher rates increase borrowing costs and reduce property valuations). Post-crash, the stock does NOT exhibit strong trending behaviour — instead it oscillates in a range with frequent oversold/overbought bounces. This makes **mean-reversion strategies** highly appropriate, while **trend-following strategies are expected to fail** in this choppy regime. The high dividend yield (6.82%) also provides income cushion during sideways periods.
