## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. `bb_meanrev_atr` — BB MeanRev (ATR Trail) — RECOMMENDED
- **Logic:** Classic mean reversion with ATR trailing stop. Enters when price touches BB Lower and RSI < 35 (oversold). Exits when price recovers to BB Middle OR hits a 4×ATR trailing stop below the 5-day high.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower(20,2) AND RSI(14) < 35
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Middle(20) OR Close < 5-day high - 4×ATR(14)
- **Results:** 58.68% return, 1.54 Sharpe, 8.48% max DD, 7.28 profit factor, 73.3% win rate, 15 trades
- **Analysis:** The clear winner. LMP.L's post-crash range-bound behaviour (170p–220p) produced frequent dips to BB Lower with RSI < 35. The BB Middle exit captured the mean-reversion bounce, while the 4×ATR trailing stop (below 5-day high) added a secondary safety net. If a bounce extended beyond BB Middle, the ATR trail kept the trade alive to capture extra gains. With 15 trades across ~4 years, this is roughly 3–4 trades per year — a steady, reliable income source. The Calmar ratio of 2.24 is strong.

### 2. `dual_exit_meanrev` — Dual-Exit MeanRev
- **Logic:** BB Mean Reversion with an added CCI filter on entry and dual exit mechanism. Entry requires BB Lower + RSI < 35 + CCI < -100 (deeply oversold on three indicators). Exit is at BB Middle OR a 3×ATR trail below 3-day high.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower(20,2) AND RSI(14) < 35 AND CCI(20) < -100
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Middle(20) OR Close < 3-day high - 3×ATR(14)
- **Results:** 49.17% return, 1.42 Sharpe, 8.48% max DD, 6.55 profit factor, 71.4% win rate, 14 trades
- **Analysis:** Nearly identical to `bb_meanrev_atr` in performance (same max DD of 8.48%), but slightly lower return. The extra CCI < -100 filter on entry reduced signal frequency (14 vs 15 trades) and filtered for slightly deeper dips. The 3×ATR trail (vs 4×ATR on the winner) was slightly tighter, causing earlier exits on extended bounces. Still an excellent strategy — the CCI filter adds confirmation but doesn't significantly improve outcomes.

### 3. `bb_meanrev_upper` — BB MeanRev (Upper Exit)
- **Logic:** Classic mean reversion — enters at BB Lower with RSI < 30 and Williams %R < -80 (deeply oversold on all three oscillators). Exits at BB Upper instead of BB Middle (captures the full band-to-band recovery).
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower(20,2) AND RSI(14) < 30 AND Williams %R(14) < -80
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Upper(20,2)
- **Results:** 36.43% return, 1.28 Sharpe, 7.43% max DD, 100% win rate, 5 trades
- **Analysis:** The key improvement over `bb_meanrev` is the BB Upper exit target. Since LMP.L oscillates in a range, dips to BB Lower are typically bought back to the upper band. Exiting at BB Upper (instead of BB Middle) captured the full band-to-band bounce. With only 5 trades and 100% win rate, the sample is small but consistent. However, the 36.43% return trails the top 2 strategies significantly — the tighter entry filter (RSI < 30 vs < 35) meant fewer opportunities.

### 4. `vol_filtered_meanrev` — Vol-Filtered MeanRev
- **Logic:** BB Mean Reversion filtered by GARCH volatility to avoid catching falling knives. Entry requires BB Lower + RSI < 35 + CCI < -100 + GARCH volatility NOT rising. Exit at BB Middle.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower(20,2) AND RSI(14) < 35 AND CCI(20) < -100 AND GARCH vol not rising
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Middle(20)
- **Results:** 20.84% return, 0.95 Sharpe, 7.03% max DD, 7.93 profit factor, 87.5% win rate, 8 trades
- **Analysis:** The GARCH volatility filter is the differentiator — it avoids entries when volatility is expanding (a sign of continuing selloff). This produced a very clean 87.5% win rate and high profit factor. However, the GARCH filter also reduced the number of signals and limited upside compared to the top strategies. The 20.84% return is solid but trails the top 3 by a wide margin.

### 5. `bb_meanrev` — BB Mean Reversion
- **Logic:** The baseline BB Mean Reversion strategy. Enters at BB Lower with RSI < 30 and Williams %R < -80. Exits at BB Middle.
- **Entry conditions:** Close ≤ BB Lower(20,2) AND RSI(14) < 30 AND Williams %R(14) < -80
- **Exit conditions:** Close ≥ BB Middle(20)
- **Results:** 16.67% return, 0.91 Sharpe, 4.44% max DD, 10.40 profit factor, 83.3% win rate, 6 trades
- **Analysis:** The baseline strategy that all improved variants are built upon. 16.67% return is respectable — it beat buy-and-hold (6.64% over the same period) by 2.5×. The 10.40 profit factor is the highest among all strategies, meaning each dollar lost was offset by $10.40 in gains. The 4.44% max DD is the tightest among the top 5, confirming conservative risk. However, the simple BB Middle exit leaves money on the table compared to the ATR trail strategies above.

## Phase 6: Final Recommendation

### Best Overall: `bb_meanrev_atr`
- **Return:** 58.68% | **Sharpe:** 1.54 | **Max DD:** 8.48% | **Calmar:** 2.24 | **Trades:** 15
- **Rationale:** Highest risk-adjusted return by a wide margin. Over the 4-year analysis period, this strategy returned 58.68% while the buy-and-hold benchmark returned only 6.64% — a 9× improvement. The Calmar ratio of 2.24 means the strategy generated 2.24× more return than its worst drawdown. The 7.28 profit factor means each dollar lost was offset by $7.28 in gains. With 15 trades across ~4 years, the signal frequency is manageable.

### Entry Rules
1. Close price must touch or fall below the lower Bollinger Band (20, 2)
2. RSI(14) must be below 35 (oversold condition)

### Exit Rules
1. **Primary:** Close recovers to Bollinger Band middle (20)
2. **Secondary:** Close falls below 5-day high - 4×ATR(14) (ATR trailing stop)

### Risk Management
- **Position sizing:** Full capital allocation per trade (consistent with backtest)
- **Stop loss:** The ATR trailing stop (below 5-day high - 4×ATR) serves as a dynamic stop loss
- **Holding period:** Variable; typically 1–10 trading days per trade (mean-reversion bounces)
- **Maximum exposure:** 1 trade at a time (signal-based)

### When This Strategy Fails
- **Sustained downtrend:** If LMP.L enters a prolonged decline (like 2022), BB Lower touches become increasingly frequent and the BB Middle exit may come too late. The ATR trail stop provides some protection but cannot prevent large losses in a crash.
- **Low-volatility consolidation:** During periods of very low volatility, the stock may not reach BB Lower frequently, resulting in long periods of no signals and cash drag.
- **Gap-down openings:** The strategy uses close prices for signals. A gap-down through BB Lower may not trigger an entry, while a gap-up through BB Middle may skip the exit price.
- **Interest rate shocks:** As a REIT, LMP.L is highly sensitive to Bank of England rate decisions. Unexpected rate hikes could cause a regime shift from mean-reversion to trend-following downside.

### Suggested Improvements
1. **Add GARCH volatility filter** — avoid entries when volatility is expanding (falling knife protection)
2. **Add volume confirmation** (CMF > -0.2) — filter out capitulation selloffs
3. **Add CCI filter** (CCI < -100) — confirm deeply oversold conditions
4. **Add regime awareness** — use HMM regime probability to reduce signals during bear regimes
5. **Use 5×ATR trail instead of 4×** — LMP.L's range-bound nature may allow wider trailing stops to capture full bounces
6. **Backtest on longer period** (2007–2026) to validate across the 2022 crash and multiple rate environments
