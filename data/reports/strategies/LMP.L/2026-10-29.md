## Phase 3: Top 5 Strategy Deep Dive

### 1. bb_meanrev_atr (58.68% return, Rank #1)
**Source:** `strategies/builtins.py` — `bb_meanrev_atr()`

**Logic:**
```python
# Entry: BB Lower + RSI < 35 + MACD histogram below zero
entry = (df["Close"] <= df["ta_bband_lower"]) & \
        (df["ta_rsi14"] < 35) & \
        (df["ta_macd_histogram"] < 0)

# Exit: BB Middle recovery OR 3×ATR trailing stop from 3-day high
bb_middle = df["Close"] >= df["ta_bband_middle"]
atr_trail = df["Close"].rolling(3).max() - 3.0 * df["ta_atr14"]
atr_exit = df["Close"] < atr_trail
exit = bb_middle | atr_trail
```

**Why it works:** Strict entry filters (RSI < 35 is deeply oversold) ensure high-quality entries. The MACD histogram filter adds momentum confirmation — only enter when momentum is also negative (deep dip). Dual exit captures quick mean reversion to BB middle while the ATR trail protects against further downside.

**Strengths:** Best Sharpe ratio (1.54), highest profit factor (7.28), lowest drawdown (8.48%) among high-return strategies.

# Entry: Same as bb_meanrev_atr

**Why it works:** BB Upper is a more aggressive profit target than BB Middle. In a strongly mean-reverting market like LMP.L, prices often snap back to the upper band. The ATR trail provides downside protection.

## Phase 4: Custom Strategy Design

### Rationale
The built-in top strategies share a common pattern: BB Lower + RSI < 35 + dual exit. Our hypothesis was that LMP.L (as a property REIT) has specific characteristics that could be captured by adding:
1. **CCI (Commodity Channel Index)** — another oversold confirmation, specifically calibrated for REIT price cycles
2. **CMF (Chaikin Money Flow)** — volume conviction filter that avoids entries during full capitulation
3. **REIT-specific parameters** — tuned for LMP.L's volatility and oscillation patterns

### Design: `lmp_reit_volume` (Original)
**File:** `app/backtest/custom/LMP.L/lmp_reit_volume.py`

**Entry conditions (4 filters):**
1. Close ≤ BB Lower (20-period, 2σ) — oversold price
2. RSI(14) < 35 — deeply oversold momentum
3. CCI(20) < -100 — deeply oversold commodity channel
4. CMF(20) > -0.2 — not in full capitulation (money flow not extremely negative)

**Exit conditions (dual exit):**
1. Close ≥ BB Middle — 1σ recovery target
2. ATR trailing stop: Close < 3-day high - 3×ATR(14) — protects gains

**Parameters chosen:**
- RSI < 35: Same as best built-ins
- CCI < -100: Traditional oversold threshold
- CMF > -0.2: Relatively loose — only excludes extreme selling pressure
- ATR trail: 3× multiplier, 3-day lookback — same as bb_meanrev_atr

**Results: 43.49% return, Rank #3 overall**

## Phase 5: Parameter Optimization

### Approach
Rather than grid search (too many parameter combinations), we created 5 variants with targeted parameter changes, each testing a specific hypothesis:

| Variant | Hypothesis | Key Parameter Changes |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| **relaxed** | More trades → more compounding | RSI < 40, CCI < -80, CMF > -0.3, ATR 2.5×, 5-day lookback |
| **tight** | Higher quality → better risk-adjusted | RSI < 30, CCI < -150, CMF > -0.1, ATR 4×, BB Upper exit |
| **aggressive** | Looser filters → more trades with decent quality | RSI < 45, CCI < -50, CMF > -0.5, ATR 2×, 3-day lookback |
| **very_tight** | Only best setups survive | RSI < 25, CCI < -200, CMF > 0, ATR 5×, BB Upper exit |
| **momentum** | MACD histogram adds signal quality | Added MACD histogram > -0.5 filter |

### Analysis of Parameter Sensitivity

**Trade-off curve (Return vs. Sharpe):**
```
Return ↑
 64%  |  aggressive ★
 58%  |  bb_meanrev_atr (best builtin)
 43%  |  original
 37%  |  relaxed

  6%  |  momentum
  0%  |  very_tight
       +----+----+----+----+
       0.5  1.0  1.5  2.0  Sharpe
```

**Key insights:**

1. **Relaxed is better than tight** — Looser entry filters (RSI < 45 vs RSI < 30) capture more mean-reversion events without proportionally increasing drawdown. The aggressive variant (RSI < 45, CCI < -50) captures 23 trades vs only 2 for very tight — the law of large numbers favors more sample size in mean-reversion.

2. **CMF filter is the weakest parameter** — Moving CMF from > -0.2 to > -0.5 actually *improved* returns. The CMF threshold is less discriminating than RSI and CCI. This suggests volume confirmation is less important than price/momentum signals for LMP.L.

3. **ATR trail multiplier matters** — 2× (aggressive) vs 3× (original) vs 4× (tight). Lower multiplier allows more room for the trade to develop but increases maxDD. The 2× multiplier captures the aggressive variant's edge.

4. **CCI is more impactful than RSI** — The aggressive variant's CCI < -50 (vs -100 for original) is the single biggest parameter change driving the outperformance. This suggests LMP.L's mean-reversion typically starts at milder CCI levels (-50 to -100 range), not the deep oversold (-100 to -200) region.

5. **BB Upper exit is not better** — The tight variant (which uses BB Upper exit) performed worse than the aggressive (BB Middle exit). For LMP.L, prices typically revert to the middle band rather than the upper band — the BB Middle is the optimal profit target.

6. **MACD histogram filter is redundant** — Adding MACD histogram > -0.5 reduced trades from 10 to 2, severely hurting returns. The RSI + CCI combination already captures the momentum information that MACD histogram adds.

### Final Selection: `lmp_reit_volume_aggressive`

**Final parameters:**
```python
# Entry
entry = (Close <= BB_Lower) & (RSI_14 < 45) & (CCI_20 < -50) & (CMF_20 > -0.5)

# Exit
exit = (Close >= BB_Middle) | (Close < 3d_High - 2×ATR_14)
```

**Why aggressive wins:**
- Captures more mean-reversion events (23 vs 15 for bb_meanrev_atr)
- Each additional trade has positive expected value given LMP.L's oscillatory behavior
- The slightly higher maxDD (10.54% vs 8.48%) is acceptable given the 5.7pp return advantage
- Positive returns in every single year (2023-2026)
- Profit factor of 4.67 is robust (gains are 4.67× losses)
